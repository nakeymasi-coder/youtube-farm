🦋 YouTube ▶ UGC Agent Farm 🦋

Your control panel for YouTube videos - Video Intake, Character Vault, solo agent jobs, and your run Queue, all in one place.

Please read this first: this dashboard is your control panel only - intake, vault, and queue. It does NOT browse, generate images, or make videos by itself. The work happens in chat from the Run in Chat prompts. NOTHING is ever posted or published automatically, ever. Publishing to YouTube always needs your separate approval.

What this farm is 🦋

One dashboard holds your Video Intake Form, a Character Vault for your locked characters, solo forms for each of your 5 agents, and a Queue for your runs. After you submit a run, your AI runs the farm WITH you in chat - YouTube research and Pinterest outfit, locked character, story and script, video, thumbnail, and your title, description and tags - and brings the finished video back for your approval.

The YouTube difference: you pick a format on every run. Long-form is 16:9 for the YouTube watch page, with a 16:9 thumbnail. A YouTube Short is 9:16. Character references stay 9:16 either way - Slot 1 headshot and Slot 2 body reference sheet are always portrait locks.

The 5 agents, in order

Agent 1 - Research Scout

Searches YouTube for proven topics, titles, hooks, and angles for the chosen mode and topic, and searches Pinterest (guest view, no sign-in) for outfits and products, especially products with an Amazon link. Picks the outfit or product and copies its picture. Does not check price or availability at this step, and never copies another creator's video - research is for the angle, the hook, and the structure only.

Agent 2 - Image Agent

Creates the person and avatar images in chat - character references, the outfit photo (your locked character wearing the exact Pinterest outfit), and the outfit reference sheet. For a Long-form run it also makes the YouTube thumbnail in chat. All ultra realistic, real skin texture and real hair texture, all PNG. Character and outfit images are 9:16. The thumbnail is 16:9.

Agent 3 - Story and Script Agent

Writes the hook, the shots, and the script. A Long-form script opens with a strong first-30-seconds hook and uses chapter breaks. A Short script is tight and hook-first. Labels every scene with the run's one Product Presentation choice. Writes scene ACTION only for video prompts - never a description of the character.

Agent 4 - Video Agent

Makes the video in chat with your AI video tool, using the Consistent Character workflow below. Long-form scenes are 16:9, Short scenes are 9:16. Returns the finished video in chat for your approval. Never posts or publishes it.

Agent 5 - Title, Description and SEO Agent

Writes the YouTube title, the full description, chapters and timestamps for Long-form, tags, and a pinned comment idea. The title stays honest - it must match what the video actually delivers. No price, discount, or sale claim unless a live price was actually verified.

YouTube Format - pick one on every run

Long-form 16:9

Scenes are built 16:9. Comes with one thumbnail: 16:9 PNG, made in chat, your locked character's real face from the references, 3 to 4 big words max, readable on a phone, no tiny text. The Title, Description and SEO Agent adds chapters and timestamps.

YouTube Short 9:16

Scenes are built 9:16, hook first, tight and fast. No thumbnail is needed - YouTube picks a frame for Shorts. Character references stay 9:16.

Character references never change with the format: Slot 1 headshot is 9:16 and Slot 2 body reference sheet is one single 9:16 photo, for both formats.

Product Presentation - one choice, never both

Every run picks ONE, right after Mode: Wearing the product OR Holding the product. Never both.

Wearing run: every scene prompt ends - "Wearing the product, not holding it."

Holding run: every scene prompt ends - "Holding the product, not wearing it."

Never show her wearing an outfit while holding that same outfit on a hanger. Reject and regenerate any image that shows both. If a video needs both, use separate scenes - holding first, wearing later - never the same shot.

The workflow - written into every run prompt

  1. Research first, in chat: the Research Scout searches YouTube for proven topics, titles, hooks, and angles for the mode and topic, and picks the outfit on Pinterest (one with an Amazon link when there is a product). Copy its picture. Never copy another creator's video.
  2. Lock the outfit IN CHAT, not in the video tool: using your locked character's reference photos, put that character in that exact outfit in chat first. Check face match and outfit match.
  3. Make the outfit reference sheet IN CHAT: from the approved outfit photo, one single 9:16 sheet with front, profile, and three-quarter shots of her in that outfit, all on the same photo. Save as PNG.
  4. Build the video with the Consistent Character lock: Reference One is her headshot (Slot 1). Reference Two is the chat-made outfit reference sheet. NEVER leave Reference Two empty. No character bible is needed - the reference photos ARE the lock. Do not paste descriptions of her into scene prompts.
  5. Create the scenes from that lock, in script order: 16:9 for Long-form, 9:16 for a YouTube Short.
  6. For Long-form, make the thumbnail in chat: one 16:9 PNG, her real face from the references, 3 to 4 big words max.
  7. Export and return the video (and thumbnail, for Long-form) IN CHAT for your approval. Never post, publish, or share it.

The scene prompt shape - use this for every scene, and nothing else about her

Use the reference images and keep the character perfectly consistent. [Scene action only.] [Spoken line, if any.] Wearing the product, not holding it.

For a Holding run, the last sentence swaps to: "Holding the product, not wearing it." NEVER describe her in a scene prompt - no face, hair, skin, body, or outfit description. Describing her overrides the references and creates a different girl.

How Run in Chat works

Every Queue card has a Run in Chat button. Pressing it sets that run to working and opens a Run in Chat card showing the locked character, Slot 1 and Slot 2 status with thumbnails when uploaded, voice, mode, Product Presentation choice and its exact prompt sentence, YouTube format, topic, length, product name and link, and reference video link when present. The card has a COPY PROMPT button. The copied prompt tells the AI to run THAT exact run with THOSE exact details, nothing swapped, following the workflow and standing rules on this page. There is no scheduled pickup.

The Queue status buttons - queued, working, done - only change the label. They never execute anything. Execution happens in chat.

Standing rules

  • Every image your AI creates is PNG, never webp. Your own uploaded Vault photos stay as you saved them (JPG or PNG).
  • Nothing is posted or published automatically, ever. Publishing to YouTube always needs your separate approval.
  • No price, discount, or sale claim in any title, description, caption, or copy unless a live price was actually verified.
  • Voice and copy everywhere: personable, human, plain words. No em dashes - use hyphens instead.
  • Within one video, identity, clothing, location, and lighting stay frozen from the reference photos.

Character Vault - add a character

One record per locked character. Each character is reusable as that same character in every run. Characters are ultra realistic, with real skin texture and real hair texture.

A character is NOT locked until BOTH slots are filled. Slot 1 is one headshot photo, 9:16. Slot 2 is ONE single 9:16 body reference sheet - not separate photos - with front, three-quarter, left profile, and right side views of the same person, same outfit, same background, all on that one photo.
